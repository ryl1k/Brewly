# Brewly FAQ

## When am I charged?
On the shipping day of each delivery, every 2 weeks. Coffee arrives 1–3 days after you're charged.

## Is shipping free?
Yes, always.

## Can I pause my subscription?
Yes, for 1 to 8 weeks. Charges stop while paused, and deliveries resume automatically.

## Can I skip a delivery?
Yes, up to 24 hours before its shipping day. Skipped deliveries aren't charged.

## How do I cancel?
Any time, no fee.
